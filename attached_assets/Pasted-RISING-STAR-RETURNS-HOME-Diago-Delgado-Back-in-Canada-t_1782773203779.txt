RISING STAR RETURNS HOME: Diago Delgado Back in Canada to Train with Kevin De Serpa, Inspires Next Generation at Ginga
KITCHENER, CANADA — The local soccer community is buzzing with excitement this week as one of its brightest talents, Diago Delgado, has officially returned to Canadian soil. The rising star is back home for an intensive training stint, linking up with renowned elite coach and former professional player Kevin de Serpa, while also taking time to give back to the grassroots community that helped shape his journey.
Delgado, whose career has been skyrocketing overseas, is using this return home to sharpen his tools and refine his game. To do that, he has turned to a trusted name in Canadian soccer development: Kevin de Serpa. Known for his tactical acumen and high-intensity player development programs, de Serpa’s training sessions are designed to push top-tier athletes to their absolute limits.
"It’s great to be back home where it all started," Delgado said during a break between training sessions. "Working with Kevin is always intense, but it’s exactly what I need to stay sharp and keep pushing my boundaries. There's nothing quite like the energy of the soccer community here."
The training regimen isn't just about grueling drills and tactical adjustments; it has also provided a golden opportunity for Delgado to connect with the next generation of football talent.
Inspiring the Kids at Ginga
Earlier today, Delgado made a special guest appearance at Ginga Soccer, surprising a group of young, aspiring players. The atmosphere was electric as the kids from Ginga realized they were sharing the pitch with a true rising star.
Delgado spent hours speaking with the youth players, sharing invaluable insights about the discipline required to play at the highest levels, answering their eager questions, and even jumping into a few skill drills. For the kids at Ginga, seeing someone who walked the same paths they are currently on achieve such success was nothing short of monumental. Smiles, autographs, and selfies dominated the afternoon, leaving an indelible mark on the academy’s young athletes.
Looking Ahead
As Delgado continues his high-performance training with de Serpa over the coming weeks, his presence serves as a powerful reminder of the talent emerging from the Canadian soccer system. For the Ginga community and local soccer fans alike, Diago’s homecoming is a celebration of how far he has come—and a thrilling glimpse into where he is headed next.
