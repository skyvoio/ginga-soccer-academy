KEVIN DE SERPA

HEAD COACH | INTERNATIONAL SOCCER PROFESSIONAL

COACHING & PLAYER DEVELOPMENT PROFILE

Kevin De Serpa is a Canadian soccer coach and former professional player whose career has taken him across Canada, Europe and Brazil.

His playing development included the Ontario Provincial Team U18 and the Canada Men's National Team pathway at U17, U19, U20 and U23 levels.

His international playing career included clubs and football environments in Portugal, Scotland, Brazil, Spain and Norway.

As a coach, Kevin has also gained international tournament experience, coaching teams at major youth competitions in Portugal and Spain, including IberCup Estoril, Barcelona, Footvia Barcelona and Challenge Cup tournaments.

⚽ COACHING PHILOSOPHY

Kevin's coaching approach is centered around developing complete players and competitive teams.

PLAYER DEVELOPMENT

Developing technical ability, decision-making, confidence and understanding of the game.

TACTICAL UNDERSTANDING

Teaching players positioning, movement, transitions, spacing and team structure.

COMPETITIVE MENTALITY

Creating an environment built around preparation, commitment, discipline, resilience and accountability.

TEAM CULTURE

Building a positive and unified environment in which players understand their roles and responsibilities.

INTERNATIONAL EXPOSURE

Using his firsthand experience in Canadian, European and Brazilian football to expose players to different styles, cultures and competitive environments.

PLAYER PATHWAY

Helping players understand what is required to progress from grassroots and youth football toward higher levels of competition.
